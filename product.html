Solving the Tokenomics Problem and the AI Factory Problem for Enterprise AI

Orchya is building the infrastructure layer that fixes how enterprises pay for AI.

As organisations scale LLM usage into production — their "AI factories" — compute and token costs scale unpredictably with them. Our system continuously optimizes token and compute allocation across models and workloads, cutting cost without cutting performance.

Who it's for

Two ways to put the optimization layer to work.

For Enterprises

Cut inference and token spend automatically

Plug in our optimization layer against your existing LLM workloads and reduce inference/token spend automatically — no re-architecture of your existing stack required.

For AI Infrastructure & Cloud Vendors

Embed cost optimization into your AI factory

Embed our cost-optimization engine directly into the AI factories you're building for your own customers — a cost layer built to sit inside someone else's platform.
