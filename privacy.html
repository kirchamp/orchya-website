What we collect

When you use the contact form on this site, we collect the information you provide: your name, email address, optional company name, and the content of your message. We do not use cookies, analytics trackers, or advertising technology on this website.

How we use it

We use the information you submit solely to respond to your enquiry and to maintain a record of business correspondence. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

We process your data under the UK GDPR legal basis of legitimate interests — responding to enquiries you have voluntarily submitted to us.

Contact form submissions are transmitted via Formspree, a third-party form-processing service, in order to deliver your message to us by email. Formspree processes this data solely to relay your submission and does not have permission from us to use it for any other purpose. No other third-party service receives data submitted through this site.

Retention

We retain contact form correspondence only as long as necessary to address your enquiry and for our legitimate business records, after which it is deleted.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, and to object to our processing of it. To exercise these rights, contact us at rskiran79@gmail.com.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to rskiran79@gmail.com or by post to our registered office above.
