    About

    A newly formed consultancy, built for the pace of change.

    Orchya Tech Limited was established to bring project, technology and human capital consulting together under one accountable, director-led team.

    / Ork-ya /

    Our story

    Orchya Tech Limited was incorporated on 05 August 2026 and is headquartered in London. We were founded on a simple premise: organisations undertaking project delivery, technology hiring, workforce transformation, or AI adoption deserve a consulting partner that moves with precision and stays accountable end to end — not a rotating cast of subcontractors.

    We work across seven connected practice areas — project consulting, tech people consulting, human capital consulting, digital & AI transformation, product development, training & certification, and events — so that clients can draw on a single trusted partner across the full lifecycle of change.

    The name is a deliberate blend: Orch, from Orchestration — coordinating people, technology and delivery as one system. Y, from Yuktira — strategic intellect and reasoning. A, from Kaushalya — skilled, decisive action. Together, Orchya: orchestration, intellect and execution, applied as a single discipline.
